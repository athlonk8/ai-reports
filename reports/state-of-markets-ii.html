State of Markets II

第二份 State of Markets，看的是 2026 年前两个季度左右的公开市场。主线很硬：科技贡献了标普 500 绝大部分盈利增长，钱从软件流进半导体和实物资产，而企业与家庭对 AI 的使用还停在很浅的一层。

1. 科技成了盈利周期本身

English

After the financial crisis, tech was the high-growth, asset-light alternative, with unused demand and software’s near-zero marginal cost. That story worked. What is new is the weight. The essay treats technology as the thing that now sets the shape of the equity cycle, the role once played by houses, cars, and other durable goods.

The number in the prose is specific: as of late August, tech accounted for about 76% of the S&P 500’s earnings growth in 2026. A companion chart is titled as tech earnings having grown 3.8× more than non-tech. That multiple is the chart’s claim, not a figure restated in the paragraph, so it is linked rather than redrawn.

中文

金融危机之后，科技是高增长、轻资产的那条路：需求还没被吃完，软件的边际成本又接近零。这条路走通了。新的地方是权重。这篇把科技看成现在决定股市周期形态的东西，过去这个角色属于房子、汽车和其他耐用品。

正文里的数字很具体：截至 8 月下旬，2026 年标普 500 的盈利增长大约有 76% 来自科技。旁边有一张图，标题主张科技盈利的增长是非科技的 3.8 倍。这个倍数只出现在图的标题里，正文没有再写一遍，所以这里只给原图链接，不重绘。

2. 从比特转向原子

English

Inside tech, the year’s rotation is from bits to atoms. Software defined the previous cycle. Hardware is now what the market is paying up for. AI construction has pulled demand into semiconductors, power, and networking — industries that used to look cyclical, sleepy, and capital intensive.

The funding path in the essay runs from the very large profits of the biggest tech companies into semiconductor free cash flow. The chart title puts it bluntly: hyperscaler free cash flow has become semiconductor free cash flow. Debt is taking a larger share of that funding. The revival is not only an AI story. The text also lists trillion-scale infrastructure needs, higher defense spending, grids dealing with electrification, reshoring, robots, and robotaxis. Public and private equity are both funding compute, memory, power, robotics, manufacturing, and defense at an intensity the author says has not been seen in decades.

中文

科技内部，这一年的轮动是从比特走向原子。上一轮的主角是软件。现在市场愿意给高价的是硬件。AI 建设把需求推进了半导体、电力和网络——这些行业过去看起来周期强、昏昏欲睡、又很吃资本。

文章里的资金路径，是最大科技公司的巨额利润，转成半导体公司的自由现金流。图的标题写得很直：超大规模云厂商的自由现金流，变成了半导体的自由现金流。债务在这笔资金里的占比正在变大。这也不只是 AI。正文还列了以万亿计的基础设施需求、上升的国防开支、被电气化压着的电网、制造业回流、机器人和无人出租车。作者认为，公开和私募市场投向算力、存储、电力、机器人、制造和防务的强度，是几十年里没有过的。

3. GPU 没有按贬值剧本走

English

On the capex itself, the claim is that compute demand is still ahead of supply. One bearish line was that GPUs would be obsolete in three or four years, so today’s B200 boom would strand the A100s installed a year or two earlier. Rental rates, and therefore residual values, were supposed to fall with age.

The essay says that has not been the pattern so far. As intelligence gets cheaper, demand for compute rises. Pricing for the newest chips has moved up, and older chips have stayed firm. Even the A100 is at or above where it was priced at the start of the year. The author’s conclusion is that better and cheaper intelligence has not been zero-sum: older chips and older models are still holding value past the dates the bears assigned.

中文

就这笔资本开支本身，文章的判断是算力需求仍快过供给。一种看空说法是，GPU 三四年就会过时，所以今天 B200 卖得再好，也会把一两年前装进去的 A100 剩成沉没资产。租金会随机龄下降，残值也应该跟着掉。

文章说，到目前为止不是这条路径。智能变便宜以后，算力需求是往上走的。最新芯片的价格在涨，旧芯片也还挺得住。即便 A100，也处在年初价格的同一水平或更高。作者的结论是，更好、更便宜的智能不是零和：旧芯片和旧模型，仍在空头给的到期日之后保持着价值。

原图：GPUs getting more valuable over time。正文没有给出各代芯片的租金序列，所以这里不补一条曲线。

4. 采用面很宽，深度很浅

English

The GPU market is already tight while adoption is still early. The prose separates two corporate disclosures: nearly 30% of S&P 500 companies report some quantifiable impact from AI, and only about 2% report a metric they actually track. The chart beside that passage uses a different cut. Its title says 69% of the S&P 500 point to a live AI deployment, and only 2% disclose a metric tracked over time. Those are not the same statistic. A live deployment is not a measured result.

On the household side, the essay says that as of April barely about 2% of U.S. households were paying for an AI service. It adds that the share is higher now and still small. Agentic use is described the same way: a small slice of users is deploying agents at any real scale. The point of the section is the gap. Hardware is already running hot before mature use has shown up in tracked numbers.

中文

GPU 市场已经偏紧，采用却还早。正文把两种公司披露分开：近 30% 的标普 500 公司报告了某种可量化的 AI 影响，只有约 2% 报告了真正在跟踪的指标。旁边那张图用的是另一刀。它的标题说，69% 的标普 500 指向一个已经上线的 AI 部署，只有 2% 披露了跨时间跟踪的指标。这不是同一个统计。上线不等于量出了结果。

家庭这边，文章说截至 4 月，大约只有 2% 的美国家庭在为某种 AI 服务付费，并且补了一句：现在更高，但仍然很小。智能体的用法也是同一判断：只有很小一截用户在有规模地部署。这一节的重点是落差。硬件已经转热，成熟使用还没出现在被跟踪的数字里。

5. 软件不是末日，是证明题

English

The year opened with a claim that software was finished, a casualty of AI that could code the product away. The essay calls the actual tape more selective than that slogan. There was a sell-off. AI, or the threat of it, is part of the story and not the whole story.

A re-rating was already due. After zero interest rates ended, companies — not only tech companies — swapped growth for profit. In 2022 the market was full of fast, mostly unprofitable software. By 2026 the mix had flipped: about 75% are profitable, and only about 30% are growing faster than 20%. Higher rates were meant to make capital scarce, so the pivot toward self-funded, slower growth was rational. Slower growth does not hold a high-growth multiple for long. Companies that are still fast still trade near historical averages, short of the zero-rate peaks, but there are fewer of them. The sector multiple came down because the mix changed. The label the essay prefers is not an apocalypse. It is a demand that software prove the growth.

中文

年初有一种说法：软件完了，AI 能把产品写出来，企业软件会被清掉。文章认为实际走势比这句口号更挑票。抛售发生过。AI，或者对 AI 的恐惧，是原因的一部分，不是全部。

重估本来就该来。零利率结束后，公司——不限于科技公司——用增长换了利润。2022 年市场上大量是高增长、多数还不赚钱的软件。到 2026 年组合反过来了：大约 75% 已经盈利，只有大约 30% 的增长还在 20% 以上。加息的用意就是让资本变稀缺，所以转向能自己养活自己、更慢的增长，是理性的。慢增长撑不久高增长倍数。仍在快速增长的公司，估值还靠近历史平均、低于零利率时的峰值，只是这样的公司变少了。板块倍数下来，是因为成分变了。文章不想用「末日」这个词。它要的是软件把增长再证明一遍。

6. 他们往前看的方向

English

Most of the presentation looks backward. The close looks forward, and the expectation is that AI widens the surface of demand: more mature use in enterprises and households, and new demand in robotics, biotech, health, and autonomous driving. The author does not claim a forecast. The claim is narrower. The technology is improving at an exponential pace, and this cycle is unlikely to resemble the ones before it.

中文

演示的大部分在回看。结尾在往前看，预期是 AI 会把需求的表面积撑大：企业和家庭里更成熟的使用，再加上机器人、生物技术、健康和自动驾驶里的新需求。作者没有假装能预报路径。判断更窄：技术在以指数速度变好，这一轮不太像以前任何一轮。
